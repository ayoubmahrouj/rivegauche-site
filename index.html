Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the trip, date and number of people. We reply with a confirmation and the pickup time.

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car. The price stays the same from 1 person up to the vehicle's capacity (usually 7).

Do I pay in advance?

No. You pay on the day, in cash (MAD or EUR) or by card to the driver.

Can you pick us up in the medina?

Yes. Cars stop at the nearest gate to your riad and the driver walks you to the door.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup.

Ready to plan your trip?

Send us a message. We reply quickly, in English or French.
