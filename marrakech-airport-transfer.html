Frequently asked questions

What if my flight is late?

We track your flight and adjust the pickup time. There is no extra charge.

How will I find the driver?

He waits in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. We also send you his WhatsApp number.

Can the car reach my riad?

Cars cannot enter the narrow streets of the medina. The driver parks at the nearest gate and walks you to the door.

How do I pay?

Cash (MAD or EUR) or card, directly to the driver.
