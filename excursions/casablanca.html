Casablanca Day Trip

⏱ 10 hours · 👥 Up to 7 people · From 150€ per car

Hassan II Mosque, the Corniche and the old medina of Morocco's largest city.

Highlights

  • Hassan II Mosque
  • Ain Diab Corniche
  • Habous quarter
  • Private car all day

Itinerary

  1. Early pickup in Marrakech
  2. Highway to Casablanca (about 3h)
  3. Hassan II Mosque and Corniche
  4. Habous quarter
  5. Return to Marrakech

Included

  • Private car or van with A/C
  • Driver (English / French)
  • Pickup and drop-off at your accommodation
  • Fuel and tolls

Not included

  • Meals and drinks
  • Entrance fees and local guides
  • Camel rides or activities (unless stated)

Frequently asked questions

Is the price per person or per car?

Per car. The price stays the same from 1 person up to the vehicle's capacity (usually 7).

Do I pay in advance?

No. You pay on the day, in cash (MAD or EUR) or by card to the driver.

Can you pick us up in the medina?

Yes. Cars stop at the nearest gate to your riad and the driver walks you to the door.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before pickup.
